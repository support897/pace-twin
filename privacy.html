Pace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Pace is a personal day planner. This policy is short because the app collects almost nothing.

What Pace collects

Nothing. Pace has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We run no server for this app, so there is nowhere for your information to be sent.

Where your information lives

Everything you put into Pace — your tasks, steps, notes, to-do items, habits, feelings check-ins and settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. If you delete the app, that information is deleted with it. We cannot see it, back it up or restore it.

Optional features

Handwriting: if you choose to scan a photo of handwriting, the reading tool is included inside Pace. Your photo is read on your device and is never uploaded by Pace.

Bringing a calendar in: if you choose to import a calendar, Pace reads the file or link you give it — once, and only to copy your events in. It never changes or deletes anything in your old calendar. If you paste a link, your device fetches that link directly from the calendar service you chose; the events then live on your device like everything else in Pace.

Voice typing: if you tap Speak, your words are turned into text by your device's own speech service (on a phone, the one built into the phone; in a browser, the browser's). That service may process the audio itself to turn your words into text; Pace collects and stores nothing. Pace asks for the microphone only when you tap Speak, keeps no recording, and the text it makes lives on your device like everything else here. Typing always works exactly the same.

Voice: if you choose to dictate, Pace uses your device or keyboard's own speech service. What that service does with your voice is covered by your device maker's policy, not ours. Typing always works instead.

Reminders: Pace's reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Children

Pace is a general-audience productivity app. It is not designed for or directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address, with a new date at the top.
