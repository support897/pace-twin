Questions, answered plainly

What does Pace Calendar cost?

Nothing. The app is free, with no card, no trial clock, and no account to create. If that ever changes, this page will say so in plain numbers before anything else does.

Where does my data live?

On your device, in the app. Pace Calendar has no server reading your calendar. You can save a backup copy from Settings at any time, and bringing that copy back restores everything.

Can I bring my old calendar in?

Yes — one step inside the app copies your events across from Google, Apple, Outlook, or a calendar file. It only ever reads; nothing in your old calendar is changed or deleted.

What phones and computers does it work on?

Any modern browser, on phone, tablet, or computer — and it can be added to your home screen like an app. Android and iPhone store versions are on their way.

What happens on a low-energy day?

Tell Pace it's a low day and the list shrinks to the few things that truly matter. Everything else waits quietly. Nothing is marked late, and nothing guilts you about it later.

How do I get help?

Through the support page — a real email answered by a real person. The same link lives inside the app under Settings, so help is never more than two taps away.
