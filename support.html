Where is my information?

On your device only. Pace has no accounts and no server. Deleting the app deletes its stored information.

Why did a reminder not arrive?

Check that reminders are on in Pace Settings and allowed in your device settings. In this version, reminders fire while Pace is installed and able to run on the device.

How does photo reading work?

The handwriting reader is built into Pace. Photos are read on your device and are not uploaded by Pace. Clear, straight-on photos work best.
